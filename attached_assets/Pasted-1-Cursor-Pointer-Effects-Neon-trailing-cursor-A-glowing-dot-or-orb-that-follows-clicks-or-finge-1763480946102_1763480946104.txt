1. Cursor & Pointer Effects

Neon trailing cursor: A glowing dot or orb that follows clicks or finger taps on mobile.

Magnetic hover effect: Elements “pull” slightly toward the cursor when hovered.

Particle explosion on click: Tiny particles burst out from the cursor on clicks/taps.

Ripple effect: Water-like ripples radiating from taps or clicks.

Blob cursor: Smooth, morphing blob that surrounds the pointer for a dynamic feel.

2. Button & UI Interactions

Glassmorphism buttons: Semi-transparent, frosted-glass buttons with subtle shadows.

Neon glow buttons: Hover or tap triggers a neon outline or flicker.

3D hover tilt: Buttons or cards tilt and cast dynamic shadows on hover.

Liquid button animations: Buttons that appear to “fill” or “flow” when hovered or clicked.

3. Loading & Transition Animations

Skeleton loading screens: Smooth placeholders while content loads.

Liquid swipe transitions: Pages or panels “flow” into each other like liquid.

Neon progress bars: Bright, glowing, animated progress indicators.

Text reveal animations: Animated gradients or glows that sweep across text.

4. Background & Hero Section Effects

Interactive particles background: Stars, dots, or shapes reacting to cursor/finger movements.

Parallax scrolling: Multi-layered backgrounds that move at different speeds.

Gradient animation: Smoothly shifting neon or pastel gradients.

Glitch effect on text/images: Slight RGB shifts and flickers for a techy vibe.

Floating neon shapes: Hovering geometric shapes with soft glows that subtly animate.

5. Icon & Microinteractions

Morphing SVG icons: Icons that smoothly transform on hover or click.

Animated checkmarks: Success indicators that draw themselves dynamically.

Bounce & scale effects: Buttons or cards subtly bounce when tapped/clicked.

Tooltip animations: Tooltips with smooth slide, fade, or blur effects.

6. Mobile-Specific

Touch ripple effects: Visual feedback for finger taps.

Haptic-like vibration effects (with animation cues): Small subtle animations indicating action.

Swipe-to-reveal menus: Smooth, interactive gestures with visual feedback.

Micro-particle trails following finger: Like neon dust or sparkles
